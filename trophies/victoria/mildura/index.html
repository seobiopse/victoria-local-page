Mildura trophy questions

Do you deliver trophies to Mildura?

Yes. Mildura is about 542 km from our Melbourne showroom, and we deliver there with standard shipping at a $9.90 flat rate or express at $25, after 5 working days production.

Can you make AFL trophies for Mildura clubs?

Yes. Afl and netball are among the main categories for Mildura, and every item includes free engraving. Popular picks include the Antique Gold Aussie Rules Figure Male On Marble Base Trophy, from $9.95. There is no minimum order.

How fast can I get trophies to Mildura for a presentation day?

Standard production is 5 working days, plus delivery to Mildura. Fast Track is 4 working days ($40 plus 10% of the order, capped at $300) and Priority Rush is 3 working days ($50 plus 15% of the order, capped at $300), also plus delivery. Tell us the event date when you order.

Can I collect my order instead of having it delivered to Mildura?

Yes, if you are coming to Melbourne. Pickup is from 11/8–20 Brock Street, Thomastown VIC 3074, Monday to Friday 9am to 5pm, with no shipping charge. Mildura is about 542 km by road from our Thomastown showroom, via the Calder Highway (A79). Google Maps shows about 6 hr 11 min in afternoon traffic.

Is engraving free?

Yes, free engraving on every order, and there is no minimum order.
