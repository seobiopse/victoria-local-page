Moe trophy questions

Do you deliver trophies to Moe?

Yes. Moe is about 152 km from our Melbourne showroom, and we deliver there with standard shipping at a $9.90 flat rate or express at $25, after 5 working days production.

Can you make AFL trophies for Moe clubs?

Yes. Afl and netball are among the main categories for Moe, and every item includes free engraving. Popular picks include the Footy Squad Trophy, from $10.00. There is no minimum order.

How fast can I get trophies to Moe for a presentation day?

Standard production is 5 working days, plus delivery to Moe. Fast Track is 4 working days ($40 plus 10% of the order, capped at $300) and Priority Rush is 3 working days ($50 plus 15% of the order, capped at $300), also plus delivery. Tell us the event date when you order.

Can I collect my order instead of having it delivered to Moe?

Yes, if you are coming to Melbourne. Pickup is from 11/8–20 Brock Street, Thomastown VIC 3074, Monday to Friday 9am to 5pm, with no shipping charge. Moe is about 152 km by road from our Thomastown showroom, via the Princes Freeway (M1). Google Maps shows about 2 hr 20 min in afternoon traffic.

Is engraving free?

Yes, free engraving on every order, and there is no minimum order.
