Richmond trophy questions

Can I pick up trophies from Thomastown if I live in Richmond?

Richmond is about 17.3 km by road from our Thomastown showroom, via St Georges Road (State Route 45). Google Maps shows about 46 min in afternoon traffic. Orders are ready for pickup at 11/8–20 Brock Street, Thomastown VIC 3074, Monday to Friday 9am to 5pm, after 5 working days production. Priority Rush pickup is available from day 3 ($15 plus 20% of the order, capped at $300). There is plenty of on-site parking.

Do you deliver trophies to Richmond?

Yes. Standard shipping to Richmond is a $9.90 flat rate and express is $25, after 5 working days production. Pickup from Thomastown has no shipping charge.

Can I order AFL trophies for a Richmond club or school?

Yes. Afl is one of the popular categories in the area, alongside cricket, tennis and basketball. A good starting point is the Global Medal 100 Games, from $6.40. Every item includes free engraving and there is no minimum order.

How fast can I get trophies for a Richmond presentation day?

Standard production is 5 working days. Fast Track is 4 working days ($40 plus 10% of the order, capped at $300) and Priority Rush is 3 working days ($50 plus 15%, capped at $300), plus delivery or pickup. Tell us the event date when you order.
