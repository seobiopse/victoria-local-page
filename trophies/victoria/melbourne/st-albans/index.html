St Albans trophy questions

Can I pick up trophies from Thomastown if I live in St Albans?

St Albans is about 23.9 km by road from our Thomastown showroom, via the M80 Ring Road. Google Maps shows about 27 min in afternoon traffic. Orders are ready for pickup at 11/8–20 Brock Street, Thomastown VIC 3074, Monday to Friday 9am to 5pm, after 5 working days production. Priority Rush pickup is available from day 3 ($15 plus 20% of the order, capped at $300). There is plenty of on-site parking.

Do you deliver trophies to St Albans?

Yes. Standard shipping to St Albans is a $9.90 flat rate and express is $25, after 5 working days production. Pickup from Thomastown has no shipping charge.

Can I order soccer trophies for a St Albans club or school?

Yes. Soccer is one of the popular categories in the area, alongside basketball, athletics and dance. A good starting point is the Mini Black Cobra Boot & Jersey Brilliance Trophy, from $8.10. Every item includes free engraving and there is no minimum order.

How fast can I get trophies for a St Albans presentation day?

Standard production is 5 working days. Fast Track is 4 working days ($40 plus 10% of the order, capped at $300) and Priority Rush is 3 working days ($50 plus 15%, capped at $300), plus delivery or pickup. Tell us the event date when you order.
